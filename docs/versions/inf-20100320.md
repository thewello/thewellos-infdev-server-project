# inf-20100320 (Infdev 20100320)

Support added after inf-20100316. Protocol number 14. SHA-256 of the original jar:
`7e6b3048a97384397e1adfb4458eafd3a0aae52845b816cfc6f138902da5e92a`.

## What the build changes (jar compared with 20100316, and the wiki page)

- The world class is called `net/minecraft/a/a/f` again (as in 20100313). Its methods moved: block `a(III)`,
  set block with notification `c(IIII)Z` (raw set `a(IIII)Z`), set metadata `b(IIII)V`, metadata `d(III)`, light `e(III)`,
  height `a(II)`, "chunk exists at this block" `b(III)Z`. The renderer listeners are in field `p`, the provider in `q`,
  the player in `a`, the time in `j` (a day is 24000 ticks).
- Chunk (`e/d`): the fields of 20100316 plus tile entities (map `k` keyed by `x + (y << 10) + (z << 20)`, chunk
  `d(III)` gets or makes the tile entity of a block). The provider interface got a third method,
  `a(provider, cx, cz)`: the populate step.
- **Terrain:** trees and ores are generated (the wiki: "re-implemented"). Ores are made when the chunk is generated; trees
  in the populate step, which writes into the chunks after its own. The game's chunk ring (`e/b`) calls populate for the
  new chunk and for the three chunks before it (x - 1, z - 1, both). The bridge does the same for new chunks
  (`TWBridge320.populateAround`). The generator code differs from 20100316, so a world taken over from 316 differs from the
  new land at the edges of the converted part.
- **Tile entities:** chests (block 54, `a/a/b/a/c`, 27 slots) and furnaces (61 and 62, `a/a/b/a/b`, 3 slots and three
  ints) exist again and keep their items. Opening them no longer crashes the game. Chests next to each other open as one
  large chest.
- **Explosions** exist: the world has an explosion method (`f.a(entity, x, y, z, power)`) that creepers and TNT use.
  **Fire** can be punched out. The starting inventory is 64 of each item (TNT instead of planks).
- Client class names of the menus, renderer and tick are unchanged: the hooks of 313 and 316 apply
  (`ClientPatcher.createTransformer320`).

## Server (`server/bridge320/TWBridge320.java`)

The bridge of 20100316 with the new names, plus:

- **Populate:** a newly generated chunk is populated like the game's ring does (see above). A chunk that was read from a
  chunk file is never populated again, also when it was taken over from an older build. Populating is limited to a nesting depth
  of six, because trees that reach into chunks that do not exist make those chunks.
- **Tile entities** are saved in the chunk file (`TileEntities`: `id` Chest or Furnace, `x`, `y`, `z`, `Items`, and
  `BurnTime`, `BurnItemTime`, `CookTime` for a furnace) and restored when the chunk is loaded. The bridge hands them to the
  server's tile system (`getTiles`, `getTileNow`, `applyTile`, `isLockableBlock`) in the format of `TileData`: a type byte
  (1 chest, 2 furnace), the slots as (short id, short count, short damage) and, for a furnace, three ints.
- The server sends the contents to the clients with each chunk, grants one lock per chest or furnace and accepts the
  contents from the player who holds the lock (the same code as for build 20100630-1340).

## Client (`client/mod320/TW320Client.java`)

The client of 20100316 with the new names, plus:

- **Chests and furnaces:** the click that opens a chest or furnace (hooked in blocks `b/a` and `b/s`) is held back until
  the server grants the lock (for every chest of a large chest). The server sends the contents first; then the click is
  repeated. When the window is closed the contents go to the server, which gives them to the other players. A furnace
  that nobody has open only runs in the games that have its chunk loaded; its contents are exchanged when it is
  opened and closed.
- **Explosions:** the explosion method is hooked at its start. The game hurts the entities of its own world; the other
  players are hurt by the mod with the game's own formula (the nearer, the more), through the server's PvP rules. The
  blocks that an explosion destroys are reported by the set-block hook like any other change.
- Items on the ground, arrows, the mobs that arrows make and the other hooks work as in 20100316.
- **Explosion burst:** the client announces an explosion with its power (`EXPLOSION`). The server then accepts the block
  changes of that explosion above the normal limit of 40 a second (up to `10 * power^3 + 100` of them in the next three
  seconds); before this, most of the destroyed blocks were refused and put back.
- **Terrain at the borders of chunks:** a chunk is only sent after the chunks around it exist, because populating a
  chunk writes into its neighbours; trees were cut off at the borders of chunks that had been sent already.

## Tested

Server start and a new world (trees, coal, iron, gold and diamond ore are generated), takeover of a 316 world (the converted chunks
are byte for byte the same in blocks), a chest opened by one client with items put in it, the items seen by another client,
saved and restored after a restart, an explosion that destroyed blocks and hurt another player.
